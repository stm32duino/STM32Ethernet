8/*
9 * Udp.cpp: Library to send/receive UDP packets with the Arduino ethernet shield.
10 * This version only offers minimal wrapping of socket.c/socket.h
11 * Drop Udp.h/.cpp into the Ethernet library directory at hardware/libraries/Ethernet/
12 *
13 * NOTE: UDP is fast, but has some important limitations (thanks to Warren Gray for mentioning these)
14 * 1) UDP does not guarantee the order in which assembled UDP packets are received. This
15 * might not happen often in practice, but in larger network topologies, a UDP
16 * packet can be received out of sequence.
17 * 2) UDP does not guard against lost packets - so packets *can* disappear without the sender being
